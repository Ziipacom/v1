# Ziipa store listing draft

Updated 2026-09-09. Proposed copy only, not a published listing. The final description must match features verified on the store build and deployed API. No screenshots, developer identity, age rating or earnings claims are invented.

**App name:** Ziipa

**iOS subtitle:** Your creative world

**Google Play short description:** Create, discover and share videos, music and live moments with Ziipa.

**Description draft:**

Create and discover in one place with Ziipa.

Explore a vertically swiped media feed, save favorites and follow your interests. Use Ziipa Studio to record with your camera or choose your own video, images and audio. Build a creation with captions, overlays, trim settings and a soundtrack, then save a private draft or publish to your Ziipa community.

- Watch media and join conversations through comments.
- Keep drafts and published creations together in your profile.
- Choose feed categories, tags and creators that interest you.
- Connect supported social accounts and see delivery status for content you choose to share. Available posting and synchronization depend on each network and its permissions.
- Use privacy settings, muted words, creator blocking and reporting controls.
- Access your Ziipa account in the mobile app and web portal.

**Conditional paragraphs — include only if verified and approved in the submitted build:**

Live: Preview your camera privately, then choose when to broadcast. Mute your microphone, switch cameras and end your stream from Ziipa. Watch available broadcasts in the Live Feed.

Web3: Connect an external wallet to use supported test-network collectible and creator-token tools. Approve wallet requests yourself. Test-network assets have no promised value; blockchain/IPFS records may remain public after account deletion. Do not present testnet assets as a funded financial product or enable unreviewed mainnet payments.
