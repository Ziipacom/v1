**Description draft:**

Find your people. Share your creativity. Make a space for every side of you.

Ziipa brings media discovery and creator tools together in one app. Explore video, music and gaming collections, keep the things that inspire you, and share creations of your own.

- Upload video, audio or images, save private drafts, and publish to the Ziipa community.
- Add a manual caption and choose playback start and end points for your media.
- Save favorites and join the conversation with comments.
- Build custom feeds using categories, tags, creators and declared cities.
- Set personal boundaries with muted words, creator blocking and content reporting.
- Use the same account in the mobile app and creator web portal.

Create something yours. Make it Ziipa.
